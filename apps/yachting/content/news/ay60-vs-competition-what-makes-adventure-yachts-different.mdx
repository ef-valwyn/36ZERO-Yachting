## The 60-Foot Power Catamaran Market

The 60-foot power catamaran segment has become one of the most competitive spaces in the marine industry. Buyers in this class are looking for a vessel that can genuinely cross oceans, live aboard for extended periods, and do so with the comfort and style expected at this price point.

Several established builders compete in this segment, each with a distinct philosophy. So where does the Adventure Yachts AY60 fit — and what genuinely sets it apart?

## Construction: Where the AY60 Leads

The single biggest differentiator is **weight**. The AY60 displaces just **18,500 kg** — a figure that puts it in a class of its own among 60-foot power catamarans.

This is not achieved by cutting corners. The AY60 uses a hybrid composite layup:

- **Carbon/epoxy** for structural elements requiring maximum stiffness-to-weight
- **eGlass/epoxy** for hull skins and secondary structures, balancing strength, impact resistance, and cost

The result is a hull that is simultaneously lighter, stiffer, and more resilient than heavier GRP or fibreglass alternatives. In practical terms, this means:

- **Better fuel economy** — less weight to push through the water
- **Higher top speeds** — the AY60 reaches 18 knots with outboard power
- **Gentler motion** — a lighter vessel responds more quickly to wave action, reducing the slow rolling that heavier catamarans can suffer from
- **Shallower draft** — just 0.9m, opening up anchorages and cruising grounds inaccessible to deeper-draft competitors

## Energy Independence

Modern cruisers increasingly want to minimise their reliance on diesel generators while at anchor. The AY60 takes this seriously:

- **8 kWp of integrated solar panels** — these are built into the vessel's design, not bolted on as an afterthought
- **1,800 Ah battery bank at 48V** — substantial capacity for air conditioning, watermakers, and domestic loads
- **Hybrid inboard option** — the Flybridge variant offers electric propulsion for silent, zero-emission manoeuvring in marinas and protected waters

At 8 knots in economy mode, the AY60 can achieve a theoretical range of **3,000 nautical miles** — enough to cross the Atlantic with reserves.

## Three Configurations, One Platform

Unlike many competitors that offer a single hull with interior options, the AY60 comes in three fundamentally different configurations:

**AY60 Sport** — Dual outboard engines deliver raw performance and simplicity. Outboards are easier and cheaper to service worldwide, and the open transom creates a large swim platform and beach club area.

**AY60 Flybridge** — Hybrid inboard propulsion with an elevated helm station. The flybridge adds a second entertaining area and provides 360-degree visibility for coastal cruising and river navigation. Range extends to 2,500+ nm.

**AY60 SportX** — The explorer variant. Optimised for extended ocean passages with additional fuel and water capacity, reinforced for blue-water conditions, and configured for owner-operator use without professional crew.

## Thai Craftsmanship

The AY60 is built in Thailand — a country with a deep maritime heritage and a growing reputation for high-quality composite boat construction.

Adventure Yachts' yard employs artisans whose families have been building boats for generations. This craftsmanship is combined with modern composite engineering, CNC-cut components, and rigorous quality control to deliver a level of fit and finish that rivals the best European yards.

Building in Thailand also means the AY60 can be offered at a price point that would be impossible for an equivalent vessel built in Europe or the United States, without compromising on materials, engineering, or quality.

## Sustainability by Design

Adventure Yachts has embedded sustainability into the AY60 from the earliest design phase:

- **Solar-first energy architecture** — reducing generator run time and fuel burn
- **Hybrid propulsion available** — electric manoeuvring in sensitive marine environments
- **Locally sourced materials** in construction and fit-out
- **Zero single-use plastics** aboard all Adventure Yachts vessels
- **Future-proofed systems** — the electrical architecture is designed to accommodate evolving battery and propulsion technologies

The 60-foot power catamaran market offers excellent options from several builders.
