## A New Chapter for Adventure Yachts

The wait is almost over. After years of meticulous design, engineering, and construction, the Adventure Yachts AY60 Sport power catamaran will make its official **World Premiere** at the International Multihull Show (Salon International du Multicoque) in La Grande Motte, France.

The show runs from **April 22-26, 2026**, and the AY60 — christened **Adventure One** — will be the centrepiece of the 36ZERO Yachting presence on the water.

## Why the AY60 Stands Out

The AY60 is not just another 60-foot power catamaran. From the ground up, Adventure Yachts set out to build a vessel that breaks with convention:

- **Lightweight composite construction** — Carbon/epoxy and eGlass/epoxy hulls bring the displacement down to just 18,500 kg, making the AY60 one of the lightest power catamarans in its class
- **Inverted bow design** — A modern hull form that cuts through swell with minimal slamming, delivering comfort on long ocean passages
- **Solar autonomy** — 8 kWp of integrated solar panels and a 1,800 Ah battery bank at 48V provide remarkable energy independence at anchor and under way
- **Range** — Over 2,000 nautical miles in Sport configuration, and up to 3,000 nm at 8 knots when optimising for economy

## Adventure One: The First of Her Kind

Adventure One is the AY60 Sport variant — the outboard-powered configuration that prioritises performance and simplicity. Key specifications include:

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Length Overall | 60 ft / 18.3m |
| Beam | 30 ft / 8.47m |
| Draft | 2.8 ft / 0.9m |
| Displacement | 18,500 kg |
| Cruising Speed | 14 knots |
| Max Speed | 18 knots |
| Range | 2,000+ nm |
| Cabins | 4+2 berths |

Built in Thailand by a team of artisans combining generations of boat-building craft with modern composite engineering, Adventure One represents the culmination of a vision that began in 2019.

## What to Expect at the Show

Visitors to the International Multihull Show 2026 will be able to:

- **Step aboard Adventure One** and experience the interior layout, build quality, and attention to detail first-hand
- **Meet the 36ZERO Yachting team** to discuss the full range of AY60 configurations — Sport, Flybridge, and SportX
- **Explore ownership options** including outright purchase, fractional ownership, and charter revenue programmes where owners can receive up to 65% of charter income

The International Multihull Show in La Grande Motte is the world's premier gathering for multihull enthusiasts, designers, and builders. With more power multihulls debuting than sailing multihulls at the 2026 edition, the show reflects the growing momentum behind the power catamaran sector — and the AY60 is arriving at exactly the right moment.
